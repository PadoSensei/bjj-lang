# Product Requirements — BJJ_Lang

**Status:** Draft  
**Version:** 0.1

## Vision
Help people understand and communicate through Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu by learning practical language, culture, and coaching instructions.

## Audiences
1. Brazilian learners, including youth, learning English through BJJ.
2. International BJJ practitioners learning Brazilian Portuguese before/during training or travel.
3. Future: academies and social-project partners.

## Initial language directions
- `pt-BR → en`: free, supported through donations.
- `en → pt-BR`: one-time paid course access.

## Core value
A learner can practice short, useful BJJ language lessons and retain vocabulary through spaced repetition.

## Product principles
- Mobile-first and intuitive.
- Fun but credible as a learning tool.
- Authentic, reviewed language and contextual examples.
- Progression inspired by BJJ belts; app belts represent language learning milestones, not real martial arts rank.
- Accessible, privacy-conscious, and especially careful with under-18 users.
- Offline-friendly practice.

## Primary journey
1. Select interface/course direction.
2. Onboard and choose a daily goal.
3. Start a beginner lesson.
4. Learn/retrieve vocabulary and phrases with context/audio.
5. Complete lesson and see progress.
6. Return for scheduled reviews.
7. Continue from words/phrases toward contextual reading and BJJ history/culture.

## Success indicators (initial hypotheses)
- Onboarding completion.
- First lesson completion.
- First review session completion.
- D1 and D7 learner return.
- Reviews completed per active learner.
- Course purchase conversion and restore success.
- Content quality reports and app crash-free sessions.

Targets should be set after beta baseline data, not invented now.

## Open product questions
- Legal/organizational recipient and accounting for donations.
- Exact purchase SKU, price, and lifetime scope.
- Parental consent and age-appropriate account flow.
- App-store release timing and availability by region.
- Initial course size and content review process.
